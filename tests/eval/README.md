**Evaluation-only sets** (IAM, BRUSH; non-commercial research terms) are **never committed**.
For local benchmarks they are fetched into RAM by `spikes/hwr/prep.py` (`nix-shell spikes/hwr/shell.nix`,
then `python -I spikes/hwr/prep.py`; writes `/dev/shm/vibenote-hwr/eval/{brush,iam}`). A proper
`cargo xtask fetch-eval` is a Phase 4 task. R-HWR-003 (English) is measured on the committed GNHK
set (`recognition/en`, owner decision 2026-10-13); IAM/BRUSH stay an optional local comparison.

Baseline 2026-10-13 (PP-OCRv6 rec small via RapidOCR/onnxruntime, greedy CTC, `spikes/hwr/evaluate.py`):
CER en (GNHK, 247 lines) 17.3 %, de (committed fhswf, 150 lines) 18.3 %, IAM test (eval-only, 400 lines)
15.2 %. Targets (R-HWR-003): en ≤ 10 %, de ≤ 12 % — red until ADR 0006 steps (a)/(b).

Regenerate `recognition/en`: download the mirror zip, unzip its `test_data.zip` to
`/dev/shm/vibenote-gnhk/ex/test`, then `nix-shell spikes/hwr/shell.nix --run "python -I tools/evalgen/make_eval.py recognition_en"`.

Owner handwriting (optional, owner 2026-10-12): may be added later as a further protected set.
